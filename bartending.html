Who We Serve

Whether you're planning a wedding, a company event, or a private party, our service is built around your specific needs — not a one-size-fits-all package.

💍

Weddings

Your wedding day shouldn't include worrying about the bar. We handle drink planning, guest flow, and on-site execution so you and your family can stay present. Our staff is trained to keep service moving, read the room, and maintain a professional presence throughout the reception. We coordinate with your venue and other vendors so the bar is one less thing on your list.

🏢

Corporate Events

Professional presentation isn't optional at a company event — it reflects on your brand. We arrive early, set up cleanly, and provide attentive, polished service that keeps your guests comfortable without drawing attention away from the event itself. From client dinners to holiday parties, we operate with the punctuality and discretion that professional settings demand.

🥂

Private Parties

Hosting is more enjoyable when you're not stuck behind the bar all night. We handle setup, service, and cleanup so you can actually enjoy your own party. Whether it's a milestone birthday, a rehearsal dinner, or a backyard gathering that calls for something elevated, we bring the bar to you and make it feel effortless from start to finish.

What's Included

Every booking includes the essentials needed for a professional, well-run bar — no hidden fees, no last-minute surprises.

✓
Professional Licensed Bartenders

Licensed, insured, and experienced in event bar service. Our staff stays professional and keeps service moving all night.

✓
Custom Cocktail Menu Consultation

Before your event, we sit down to plan a drink menu that fits your guests, your theme, and your vision.

✓
Setup and Breakdown

We arrive ahead of your event to set up completely and handle all breakdown after — so you're not left cleaning up a bar at the end of the night.

✓
All Bar Equipment and Tools

Bar tools, ice service, bar mats, pourers, shakers, and everything needed for professional service. No need to source equipment separately.

✓
Non-Alcoholic Options

Every bar includes thoughtfully considered non-alcoholic service so all of your guests have something to enjoy — not just a glass of water.

✓
Guest-Count-Based Staffing

We staff each event based on your guest count so service stays smooth throughout the event, not just the first hour.

Staffing guidance: Typically 1 bartender serves up to 50 guests, 2 bartenders serve up to 100 guests, with additional staff recommended for larger events. We'll confirm the right staffing level during your consultation.
